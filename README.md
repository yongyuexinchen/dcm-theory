## What is this?

The Death Constraint Model (DCM) addresses how **individuals** respond when death pressure meets desire. Companion theory to EGS (Existential Guarantee System theory): where EGS addresses how societies change when existential guarantees fail, DCM addresses the individual-level dynamics of desire under mortality constraints.

**Intellectual lineage:** extends Deleuze & Guattari's desire production theory (not Freud's death drive) — desire is production constrained by mortality.

## Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Status |
|---|-----------|--------|
| H-A | Mortality salience increases normative compliance (desire encoding), moderated by perceived social enforcement | Testable |
| H-B | "Desire quenching": preemptive withdrawal of desire as the desired object approaches realization | Testable (origin: n=1, needs replication) |

## Relationship to EGS

```
Death pressure (high)
    ↓
H-A: Desire encoded into normative compliance
    ↓
EGS fulfillment declines ("normal" no longer delivers)
    ↓
H-B: Desire quenches before disappointment
    ↓
Escape lines (future work)
```

The two frameworks are theoretically related but **empirically separable** — each can fail independently without invalidating the other.
